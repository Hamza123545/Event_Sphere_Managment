## Getting Started

### Registration

1. Navigate to the EventSphere registration page
2. Fill in your information:
   - Email address
   - Password
   - First and last name
   - Phone number (optional)
3. Select role: **Exhibitor**
4. Accept terms and conditions
5. Click "Register"

### Login

1. Go to the login page
2. Enter your email and password
3. Click "Login"

### Dashboard Overview

After logging in, you'll see your Exhibitor Dashboard with:
- Your registered expos
- Pending applications
- Messages and notifications
- Quick actions

## Registering for an Expo

### Browsing Available Expos

1. Click "Browse Expos" in the main menu
2. View list of available expos
3. Use filters to find relevant expos:
   - **Category**: Filter by expo theme
   - **Location**: Search by city or country
   - **Date**: Filter by date range

### Viewing Expo Details

1. Click on an expo to view details
2. Review:
   - Event description and theme
   - Date and location
   - Organizer information
   - Registration deadline

### Submitting Application

1. Click "Register as Exhibitor" button
2. Fill in your exhibitor profile:
   - **Company Name**: Your company or organization name
   - **Category**: Industry category
   - **Description**: Company description and offerings
   - **Website**: Company website URL
   - **Contact Email**: Business contact email
   - **Contact Phone**: Business phone number
   - **Company Size**: Number of employees
   - **Products/Services**: List of products or services you'll showcase
3. Click "Submit Application"

### Application Status

Your application status will be one of:
- **Pending**: Under review by organizer
- **Approved**: Accepted! You can now select a booth
- **Rejected**: Application was not approved (contact organizer for details)

## Managing Your Profile

### Updating Profile Information

1. Navigate to "Settings" → "Profile"
2. Update any information:
   - Company details
   - Contact information
   - Products/services
   - Company size
3. Click "Save Changes"

### Multiple Expo Profiles

You can have different exhibitor profiles for different expos:
- Each expo registration creates a separate profile
- Profile information is specific to each expo
- Update each profile independently

## Selecting a Booth

### Viewing Available Booths

1. Once your application is approved, navigate to your expo
2. Click "Floor Plan" tab
3. View interactive floor plan showing:
   - **Available Booths**: Green (available to reserve)
   - **Reserved Booths**: Yellow (reserved by other exhibitors)
   - **Your Booth**: Blue (if already reserved)

### Booth Information

Hover over a booth to see:
- **Identifier**: Booth number/name
- **Size**: Dimensions and area
- **Amenities**: Available features (power, internet, etc.)
- **Price Tier**: Standard, Premium, or Deluxe

### Reserving a Booth

1. Click on an available booth
2. Review booth details
3. Click "Reserve This Booth"
4. Confirm your selection
5. Your booth is now reserved!

### Changing Your Booth

1. If you need to change your booth:
   - Contact the organizer via messages
   - Explain your request
   - Organizer can reassign your booth

## Viewing Floor Plans

### Interactive Floor Plan

1. The floor plan shows the entire expo layout
2. Use zoom controls to get a closer look
3. Click on booths to see details
4. Your reserved booth is highlighted

### Booth Details

Each booth displays:
- Location coordinates
- Size and area
- Amenities included
- Price tier information

## Communicating with Organizers

### Sending Messages

1. Navigate to "Messages" in the main menu
2. Click "Compose Message"
3. Select recipient (organizer)
4. Choose context:
   - **General Inquiry**: General questions
   - **Exhibitor Collaboration**: Business-related
   - **Support Request**: Technical or account issues
5. Enter subject and message
6. Click "Send"

### Receiving Messages

1. Check "Messages" for new messages
2. Messages appear in your inbox
3. Click to read and reply
4. Messages related to your expo registration are tagged

### Notifications

You'll receive notifications for:
- Application approval/rejection
- Booth assignment
- Schedule changes
- New messages
- Important expo updates

## Tips and Best Practices

1. **Complete Your Profile**: Detailed profiles are more likely to be approved
2. **Apply Early**: Popular expos fill up quickly
3. **Choose Carefully**: Select a booth that fits your needs and budget
4. **Stay Updated**: Check messages and notifications regularly
5. **Be Professional**: Maintain clear communication with organizers

## Troubleshooting

### Application not approved?

- Review organizer feedback (if provided)
- Ensure all required information is complete
- Contact organizer via messages for clarification
- Check if expo is still accepting applications

### Can't see floor plan?

- Verify your application is approved
- Check that organizer has created a floor plan
- Refresh the page
- Contact support if issues persist

### Need to cancel registration?

- Contact the organizer via messages
- Explain your situation
- Organizer can process the cancellation
