## Getting Started

### Registration

1. Navigate to the EventSphere registration page
2. Fill in your information:
   - Email address
   - Password
   - First and last name
   - Phone number (optional)
3. Select role: **Attendee**
4. Accept terms and conditions
5. Click "Register"

### Login

1. Go to the login page
2. Enter your email and password
3. Click "Login"

### Dashboard Overview

After logging in, you'll see your Attendee Dashboard with:
- Your registered expos
- Upcoming sessions
- Personal schedule
- Recent activity

## Browsing Expos

### Finding Expos

1. Click "Browse Expos" in the main menu
2. View list of available expos
3. Use filters to find what you're looking for:
   - **Status**: Upcoming or Active expos
   - **Category**: Filter by theme or category
   - **Location**: Search by city or country
   - **Date**: Filter by date range

### Expo Details

Click on an expo to see:
- **Overview**: Description and theme
- **Schedule**: All sessions and events
- **Exhibitors**: List of participating exhibitors
- **Floor Plan**: Expo layout and booth locations
- **Location**: Venue information and directions

## Registering for an Expo

### Registration Process

1. Browse expos and find one you're interested in
2. Click "Register for Expo" button
3. Fill in registration information:
   - **Interests**: Select topics you're interested in
   - **Dietary Restrictions**: Any special dietary needs (optional)
4. Click "Register"

### Registration Confirmation

- You'll receive a confirmation message
- The expo appears in your dashboard
- You can now access all expo features

### Viewing Your Registrations

1. Go to your Dashboard
2. See all expos you've registered for
3. Click on an expo to access:
   - Schedule
   - Exhibitor directory
   - Floor plan
   - Your personalized schedule

## Searching Exhibitors

### Exhibitor Directory

1. Navigate to an expo → "Exhibitors" tab
2. Browse all participating exhibitors
3. View exhibitor profiles:
   - Company name and description
   - Category and products
   - Booth location (if assigned)
   - Contact information

### Search Options

Use search filters to find exhibitors:
- **Category**: Filter by industry or category
- **Company Name**: Search by company name
- **Product Keyword**: Search for specific products or services

### Bookmarking Exhibitors

1. Find an exhibitor you're interested in
2. Click the bookmark icon
3. Bookmarked exhibitors appear in your favorites list
4. Visit their booth during the expo!

## Managing Your Schedule

### Personal Schedule View

1. Navigate to "My Schedule" in the main menu
2. View all your bookmarked sessions organized by:
   - **Day**: Sessions grouped by date
   - **Time**: Chronological order
   - **Category**: Organized by session type

### Session Details

Each session shows:
- **Title and Description**: What the session is about
- **Time**: Start and end time
- **Location**: Room name and capacity
- **Speaker(s)**: Presenter information
- **Bookmark Status**: Whether you've bookmarked it

### Schedule Conflicts

The system will warn you if you have:
- Overlapping sessions
- Sessions too close together
- Too many sessions in one day

Resolve conflicts by unbookmarking sessions or choosing alternatives.

## Bookmarking Sessions

### Adding Sessions to Schedule

1. Browse the expo schedule
2. Find sessions you're interested in
3. Click the "Bookmark" button (bookmark icon)
4. Session is added to your personal schedule
5. You'll receive reminders before the session starts

### Reminder Preferences

1. Go to "My Schedule"
2. Click on a bookmarked session
3. Configure reminder settings:
   - **Enabled**: Turn reminders on/off
   - **Reminder Time**: Minutes before session (5-1440 minutes)
   - **Channels**: Email and/or in-app notifications
4. Click "Save Preferences"

### Removing Bookmarks

1. Go to "My Schedule"
2. Find the session you want to remove
3. Click "Remove Bookmark"
4. Session is removed from your schedule

## Viewing Floor Plans

### Interactive Floor Plan

1. Navigate to an expo → "Floor Plan" tab
2. View the entire expo layout
3. Interactive features:
   - **Zoom**: Get a closer look at specific areas
   - **Booth Details**: Click on booths to see information
   - **Your Bookmarks**: Your bookmarked exhibitors are highlighted

### Finding Exhibitor Booths

1. Use the floor plan to locate exhibitors
2. Bookmarked exhibitors are highlighted
3. Click on a booth to see:
   - Exhibitor information
   - Booth amenities
   - Quick link to exhibitor profile

### Navigation Tips

- Use the zoom controls for detailed views
- Search for booth numbers or exhibitor names
- Legend shows booth status (available, reserved, occupied)

## Tips and Best Practices

1. **Plan Ahead**: Bookmark sessions early to secure your spot
2. **Balance Your Schedule**: Don't overbook - leave time for networking
3. **Explore Exhibitors**: Research exhibitors before the expo
4. **Set Reminders**: Configure reminders so you don't miss important sessions
5. **Stay Updated**: Check for schedule changes and expo updates

## Troubleshooting

### Can't register for an expo?

- Check if registration is still open
- Verify expo status (must be "upcoming" or "active")
- Ensure you're logged in
- Contact organizer if issues persist

### Session bookmark not working?

- Verify you're registered for the expo
- Check if session is still available
- Refresh the page
- Contact support if problem continues

### Can't see floor plan?

- Ensure you're registered for the expo
- Check that organizer has created a floor plan
- Try refreshing the page
- Contact support if needed
